# 🌹 Birthday Experience for Shahd | Intro & Audio Experience

A clean, high-end, and aesthetic birthday landing page designed for Shahd with custom media playback and theater transitions.

---

### ✨ Features & Specifications:

1. **Assets & Media Integration**:
   - **Audio File**: `Lovesong.mp3` (Adele - Lovesong)
   - **Cover Art**: `Lovesong.jpg` (Album Artwork)
   - **Curtain Transition**: `output-onlinegiftools.gif` (Custom watercolor blooming flower)
   - **Stickers**: `Love You Cat Sticker.gif` & `Cat Party Sticker.gif`

2. **Retro Y2K Spinning CD Player Component**:
   - Displays `Lovesong.jpg` on the face of an authentic CD with holographic sheen reflections and clear acrylic casing.
   - When the user presses **"Start"**:
     1. `Lovesong.mp3` begins playing directly.
     2. The CD activates its continuous spinning animation.
     3. The floral split-curtain transition (`output-onlinegiftools.gif`) sweeps in and slides open sideways to reveal the **"Coming Soon... 🌹"** page.
     4. `Lovesong.mp3` plays seamlessly and continuously across the background without interruption.

3. **Responsive & Mobile-Ready**:
   - 100% fluid scaling, clean touch targets, and zero horizontal scroll on mobile (iOS & Android) and desktop displays.
